## Full Content & Structured Data

### Fund Structure & Objectives
- **Issuer**: State Street Global Advisors (SSGA).
- **Trustee**: State Street Bank and Trust Company.
- **Replication Strategy**: Full replication of the S&P 500 Index (500 leading large-cap US equities).
- **Expense Ratio**: 0.0945% per annum.

### Creation & Redemption Mechanics
SPY creates and redeems shares in Creation Units (typically 50,000 shares) in-kind with Authorized Participants (APs). This institutional arbitrage mechanism keeps market price tightly anchored to the Net Asset Value (NAV).

### Regulatory Note for European / UK Retail Accounts
Under UK/EU PRIIPs regulations, retail investors cannot buy direct US-domiciled ETFs like SPY in standard investment accounts. Retail execution must route through UCITS equivalents (`VUSA.L` or `VUAG.L`).
